01 / THE ORGANIZATION

About ASME.

The American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) is a professional organization that supports engineering communities through knowledge sharing, professional development, collaboration, and engagement with engineering.

Through a broad professional community, ASME connects people who are interested in learning, exchanging ideas, and contributing to engineering.

02 / OUR STUDENT COMMUNITY

ASME at the
University of Jordan.

The ASME Student Section at the University of Jordan brings together students who are interested in engineering, creativity, collaboration, and professional growth. It provides a community where students can explore ideas, contribute their skills, learn from one another, and participate in activities that complement their academic journey.

You do not need to have every answer before joining the conversation. Curiosity, commitment, and a willingness to contribute are good places to start.
